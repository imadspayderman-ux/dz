# 3D Model Credits

This directory bundles real, artist-made 3D car models that are freely
licensed. Every car you see in the showroom is one of these.

## models/kenney/ — Kenney Car Kit

- **Source:** [kenney.nl — Car Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit) by **Kenney**
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain) —
  <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- **Files:** ambulance, delivery, delivery-flat, firetruck, garbage-truck,
  hatchback-sports, police, race, race-future, sedan, sedan-sports, suv,
  suv-luxury, taxi, tractor, tractor-police, tractor-shovel, truck,
  truck-flat, van — 20 vehicles total.
- **Note:** No attribution is required by the licence, but Kenney is
  credited here as a courtesy.

## models/threejs/ — Three.js example asset

- **Source:** [Three.js examples — `models/gltf/ferrari.glb`](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/examples/models/gltf)
- **License:** MIT (the Three.js project licence applies to its example
  assets) — <https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE>
- **Files:** `ferrari.glb` — a stylised Ferrari 458 model used in the
  Three.js `webgl_materials_car` demo.

## models/khronos/ — Khronos sample assets

- **Source:** [KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets)
- **License:** CC-BY 4.0 — attribution required.
- **Files:** `CarConcept.glb` — concept hyper-car by Mathias Bonnevay /
  Khronos Group. `ToyCar.glb` — toy car by 2008 Pixar (CC-BY).
